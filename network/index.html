Surrogate: Actor Network Diagram

This diagram shows all the people, ideas, and events connected to Surrogate, a project by artist Lauren Lee McCarthy.

At the center are Lauren and the project itself. Around them are doctors, parents, technologies, laws, and viewers who were part of making or reacting to the work.

The size of each box shows how important the person or idea is. The farther away something is, the later it came in the process — from inspiration to creation to public reaction.
